Solo build

LesPaulverizer

ESP32 jam box on a custom PCB: mixes up to 4 looping backing tracks from microSD, with an op-amp output stage that plugs into any amp.

Role
Solo, from schematic to firmware

ESP32
Audio

I2C LCD · rotary encoder · play/pause
Power
9 V battery · LM2596 buck

What it doesA backing band in a box

Load backing tracks onto a microSD card, pick up to four with the rotary encoder and LCD, and they loop together while you play over them. One button pauses and resumes. Plug it into any amp and jam by yourself.

DemoHear it play

Full demo with sound: selecting tracks with the encoder and starting playback with the button.

Digital sideFrom SD card to I2S

Tracks live on the microSD card as WAV files. The ESP32 reads them over SPI, mixes the active ones together in software, and streams the result over I2S to a MAX98357A amplifier. The LCD runs over I2C, with the encoder and button on GPIO.

Everything sits on a custom carrier board I designed: off-the-shelf modules for the ESP32, SD card, amplifier and buck converter, plus my own through-hole op-amp output stage.

Analog sideMaking a speaker amp talk to a guitar amp

The MAX98357A is built to drive a speaker directly, so its output is differential. I filter both outputs with RC low-pass stages, then a TL072 difference amplifier converts the signal to single-ended around a 4.5 V virtual ground, so everything runs from one 9 V battery. A second TL072 stage buffers the signal out to the jack.

SchematicThe full circuit

Power, the ESP32 and its peripherals, and the amplifier and op-amp output stage.

Hardest problemFinding an op-amp good enough for audio

The problem

The MAX98357A is designed to drive a low-impedance speaker with a differential output. A guitar amp expects a single-ended signal into a high-impedance input. Plugging one into the other directly doesn't work.

The approach

An op-amp stage could match the two: a difference amplifier to make the signal single-ended around a 4.5 V virtual ground, and a buffer to drive the jack. But not every op-amp keeps audio clean, and the wrong one adds noise and distortion you can hear.

The fix

After comparing options, I settled on the TL072, a JFET-input op-amp built for audio. Its high input impedance and low noise gave a clean signal the amp can take directly.
