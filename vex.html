Solo build

VEX U Robot

VEX U Tipping Point robot: ring intake, four-bar mobile-goal lift with pneumatic front and rear clamps, 10-motor drive, and custom carbon-fiber polycarbonate parts.

Role
Solo: design, build and programming
Team
WPI VEX U, team GOATS
Season
2021–22, Tipping Point
Drive
10 motors
Code
VEXcode extension in VS Code

MechanismsGrab a goal, then fill it

A front clamp on a four-bar lift grabs and raises mobile goals. A rear clamp holds a second goal while the ring intake loads rings onto it. Both clamps are pneumatic, fed by the onboard air tanks. A 10-motor drive moves it all around the field.

DriveTen motors on the ground

Each side of the drive runs five motors geared together, driving a row of omni wheels.

Custom partsModding the printer to print the parts

VEX U allows custom fabricated parts, so I printed key components in carbon-fiber polycarbonate. My Ender 3 couldn't handle that filament, so I upgraded it: a new hotend, a new control board, and Marlin firmware flashed onto it.

CompetitionBuilt, driven and coded solo

I designed, built and programmed the robot alone and competed with it for WPI's VEX U team, GOATS, in the 2021–22 season.
